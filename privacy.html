Privacy notice

Preview version · Last updated 26 November 2026

This is a demonstration website, not a live social network. Do not submit real personal, contact, identity or sensitive information.

What this preview stores

When you create a demo post, the text and a local date/time are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. The preview does not send that post to a Mzansi-Pages server. You can clear this preview data by clearing this site's browser storage.

Accounts and tracking

Real user registration, authentication, private messaging, photo uploads, analytics and advertising are not connected in this preview. Do not assume that a profile, like or connection action is shared with another person.

Children and age eligibility

The proposed first release is intended for adults aged 18 and older. A date-of-birth screen in a prototype is not age verification. A production service must implement appropriate age assurance and enforce eligibility on its server before creating accounts or providing access.

Production privacy notice

Before a public service launches, its operator must replace this preview notice with a legally reviewed notice identifying the responsible operator, lawful purposes, data categories, recipients, retention periods, security practices, user rights, contact details and applicable South African POPIA processes. Do not publish this placeholder as the final legal notice.
