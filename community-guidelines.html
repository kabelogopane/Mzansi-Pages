Community guidelines

Preview policy · Last updated 26 November 2026

These are initial draft guidelines for product planning. Reporting, moderation and enforcement tools are not active in the preview.

Our community principles

  • Treat people with respect. Harassment, threats, hate and targeted abuse are not welcome.
  • Respect privacy. Do not share another person's private information, images or conversations without permission.
  • Keep content honest. Do not impersonate people, run scams or deliberately spread harmful misinformation.
  • Do not spam, manipulate engagement or misuse the platform.
  • Respect intellectual property and only share content you have the right to use.
  • Use appropriate reporting channels when content or behaviour creates a concern. Do not retaliate.

Safety and age

The planned first release is for verified adults aged 18 and older. Dating is intended to be a separate optional adults-only area, unavailable unless reliable age assurance and additional safety controls are in place. The current preview does not provide dating, matching or dating messages.

Moderation before launch

A real launch requires staffed moderation, report review, blocking, appeals, escalation procedures, repeat-offender controls and clear response targets. These controls are not implemented by this static preview.

Enforcement

In a production service, content or accounts that violate published rules may be restricted or removed after a fair review process. Emergency safety reports need a clear escalation path.
